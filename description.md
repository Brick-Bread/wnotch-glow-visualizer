Turns the glow around the pill into a **music visualiser** that follows whatever your PC is playing.

Eight animations: **Pulse**, **Spectrum**, **Mirror**, **Wave**, **Comet**, **Fire**, **Strobe** and **Aurora**, or **Auto** to rotate through them. Choose a rainbow, your accent colour or any single colour, and set the sensitivity, all from Notch's Settings.

It reads only the *loudness by pitch* of the sound output, never the sound itself, and only while the plugin is switched on. When nothing is playing it steps aside and the normal glow returns. Needs Notch 0.12 or newer.
